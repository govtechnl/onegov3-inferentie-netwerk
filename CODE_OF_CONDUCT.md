# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Handling data, capacity information and credentials

This challenge is about real organisations and their infrastructure. Some extra rules apply:

- **Simulated only.** Do not put real requests, personal data, tax data, internal documents or real capacity, usage or cost figures of any organisation in this repository, in issues, or on Alkemio. If a challenge owner or lawyer tells your team something in confidence, it stays out of your submission.
- **No credentials.** Never commit API keys, master keys or tokens, including GreenPT credentials. Use environment variables and keep `.env` files out of git.
- **Honest labelling.** Do not present a simulated connection, capacity figure or legal construction as real. Say what is simulated and what is assumed.
- **No verdicts on real parties.** The fictitious organisations in `data/` stand for roles. Do not present your results as a judgement about how a real organisation runs its infrastructure.
- **Respect for the people involved.** Challenge owners and lawyers give their time to help teams. Their input during the hackathon is not a formal position of their organisation.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the organisers at [hack@govtechnl.nl](mailto:hack@govtechnl.nl) or to any organiser on site. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Organisers may remove contributions or exclude participants who violate this code.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines, see <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
